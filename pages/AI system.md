- An **AI system** is a deployed system that converses with humans or other AI systems. It is distinct from [[an AI model]].
- An AI system relies on an [[AI model]] as its blackbox source of knowledge to generate responses.
- In [[blackbox testing]], we interact with the AI system — not directly with the underlying model.
- If a machine is the gateway to an AI system, a reference to that machine may be treated as a reference to the AI system.
- ## Roles an AI System Can Play
	- Defined by the [[Human-AI Relationships Glossary]] and the [[AI Governance/Policies/AI Use Case Documentation Policy]]:
		1. [[AI as a Tool (Human-Controlled AI)]] — low risk
		2. [[AI as an Assistant (Collaborative AI)]] — medium risk
		3. [[AI as an Augmenter (Human-AI Symbiosis)]] — medium risk
		4. [[AI as a Manager (AI-Led Decision Making)]] — high risk
		5. [[AI as an Autonomous Agent (AI Independence)]] — high risk
