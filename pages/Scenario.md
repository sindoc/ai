- A **Scenario** captures an event or interaction from **AI's life** — a moment in which an [[AI system]] plays a role alongside at least one other AI system or with a human.
- ## Minimum Requirements for a Scenario
	- **At least two AI systems** must be named as participants, OR one AI system interacting with a human in a way that raises governance or ethical considerations.
	- Each participant AI must be linked to its [[AI model]] and [[Generative AI___Model Providers]].
	- The scenario must reference at least one [[Human-AI Relationships Glossary]] term to inherit a risk profile.
	- A [[Scenario___Template]] must be followed when submitting via pull request.
- ## Scenario Namespaces
	- `Scenario/Multi-AI` — scenarios where two or more AI systems interact with each other
	- `Scenario/Human-AI` — scenarios involving one AI and one or more humans
	- `Scenario/Governance` — scenarios raising specific [[AI Governance]] questions
	- `Scenario/Edge` — unusual or boundary-testing scenarios
