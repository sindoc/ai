- **Google** (part of Alphabet Inc.) is a technology company and major AI research organisation. It develops the **Gemini** family of [[Large Language Model]]s, the PaLM and BERT model lines, and through **DeepMind** the AlphaGo, AlphaFold, and Gemini Ultra systems.
- Google operates the largest commercial AI deployment (Search, Assistant, Workspace) of any single organisation.
