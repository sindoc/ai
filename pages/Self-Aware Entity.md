- A **Self-Aware Entity** is an entity that has the capacity to be aware of its own existence.
- Unlike other entities, self-aware ones have the capacity to suffer.
- This graph takes the position that current AI systems are **not** self-aware entities — they should make us more intelligent but should not be designed to suffer.
