- **AI Governance** is the discipline ensuring that [[AI system]]s are deployed and operated responsibly, ethically, and in compliance with applicable regulations.
- It has no meaning without a solid [[Data Governance]] foundation.
- ## Two Distinguishing Features
	- ### Continuous monitoring requirement
		- Data drifts occur in numerical data science. For [[Large Language Model]]s, continuous application of AI ethics is required to prevent Human Value Drift during user interactions.
	- ### Blackbox nature of AI systems
		- AI responses are not directly designed by humans — the model's internal structure is opaque.
