- ## 1. Purpose
	- Establish a framework for documenting all AI use cases, linking each to the [[Human-AI Relationships Glossary]] to automatically inherit the associated risk profile.
- ## 2. Scope
	- All AI projects and [[Scenario]]s, including those submitted as pull requests to this graph.
- ## 3. AI Role Classification
	- Every scenario or use case must declare the AI role using one of the five terms from [[Human-AI Relationships Glossary]]:
		1. **AI as a Tool (Human-Controlled AI)** — low risk
		2. **AI as an Assistant (Collaborative AI)** — medium risk
		3. **AI as an Augmenter (Human-AI Symbiosis)** — medium risk
		4. **AI as a Manager (AI-Led Decision Making)** — high risk
		5. **AI as an Autonomous Agent (AI Independence)** — high risk
- ## 4. Risk Profile Inheritance
	- Linking a scenario to the correct glossary term automatically inherits the risk tier.
	- High-risk scenarios require additional governance review before merging.
