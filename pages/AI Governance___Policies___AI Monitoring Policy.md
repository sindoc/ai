- ## Purpose
	- Ensure that all [[AI system]]s referenced in this graph are monitored for data drift, value drift, and bias — especially in [[Scenario]]s involving multi-AI interaction.
- ## Key Requirements
	- Scenarios tagged as high-risk must link to a monitoring approach or tool from [[AI Governance/Tools]].
	- [[Large Language Model]]-based scenarios must address [[Human Value Drift]] risk.
