- **Mistral AI** is a French AI company founded in 2023 by former researchers from [[Meta]] and [[Google]] DeepMind. It develops open-weight and commercial [[Large Language Model]]s, including the Mistral 7B, Mixtral (MoE), and Mistral Large families.
- Mistral AI models are notable for strong performance relative to parameter count and for their open-weight release policy.
