- **Singine** is the core platform (`~/ws/singine`) for subscriber management, content, and monetization — and also hosts the canonical registry for [[Asset Codes]].
- Within this graph, Singine is used to:
	- Manage and validate four-letter [[Asset Codes]] (uniqueness, retrieval, registration)
	- Support file transformation in conjunction with [[SilkPage]]
