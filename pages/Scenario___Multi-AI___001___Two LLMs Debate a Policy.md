- ### Description
	- Two large language model systems are each asked by separate human operators to argue for and against an AI governance policy (e.g., mandatory impact assessments for high-risk AI).
	- The outputs are then compared — not by the AIs themselves, but by a human arbitrator — to surface points of agreement and disagreement between the two models' "positions".
