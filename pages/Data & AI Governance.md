- **Data & AI Governance** is the organisational discipline that combines [[Data Governance]] (tactical/strategic data operations) with [[AI Governance]] (operational monitoring and ethics for AI systems).
- AI Governance has no meaning without a solid Data Governance infrastructure beneath it.
- ## Two Levels
	- **Data Governance**: aligns data operations at tactical and strategic levels with company objectives; mitigates data risks.
	- **AI Governance**: operationalises the novel requirements of continuously running, blackbox AI systems.
