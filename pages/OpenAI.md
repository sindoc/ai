- **OpenAI** is an AI research organisation founded in 2015 and restructured as a capped-profit company. It develops the GPT family of [[Large Language Model]]s (including GPT-4), the DALL-E image models, and the Codex code generation model.
- OpenAI operates both a research division and a commercial API platform.
