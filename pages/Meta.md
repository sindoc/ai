- **Meta** (formerly Facebook) is a technology conglomerate and AI research organisation. It develops the **Llama** family of open-weight [[Large Language Model]]s, released under a community licence, as well as research into multimodal AI, robotics, and AR/VR.
- Meta AI Research (FAIR) publishes extensively in open AI research.
