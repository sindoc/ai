title:: AI Governance/Sensitive Attributes
asset-code:: SNSA
public:: true

- **Sensitive Attributes** are demographic, behavioural, or psychological characteristics that carry inherent bias risk when used in or by [[AI system]]s.
- ## Categories
	- [[AI Governance/Sensitive Attributes/Demographic]] — age, gender, ethnicity, nationality
	- [[AI Governance/Sensitive Attributes/Behavioural or Psychological]] — personality, belief, emotional state
	- [[AI Governance/Sensitive Attributes/Cultural & Social]] — cultural markers, social norms, religious affiliation
- ## Relevance to Scenarios
	- Any [[Scenario]] that involves an AI system handling, inferring, or producing content about sensitive attributes must flag this in its governance section and link to the relevant sub-page here.
